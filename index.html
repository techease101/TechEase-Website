QUESTIONS

A few things to know.

Do you work with both homes and businesses?

Yes. TechEase supports individuals, households, home offices and small businesses with everyday technology, networking and audio/video needs.

Can you help if I don't know what the problem is?

Yes. Describe the symptom — slow computer, weak Wi-Fi, no sound, blank screen, app errors or devices that won't connect — and we can begin with diagnosis.

Do you offer remote support?

Yes, when the issue can be safely handled without physical access to the equipment. Hardware installation and hands-on troubleshooting require an on-site visit.

Is support available in Spanish?

Yes. TechEase provides support in both English and Spanish.
