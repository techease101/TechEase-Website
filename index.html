FAQ

Before you book.

Do you work with both homes and businesses?

Yes. TechEase supports individuals, home offices, small businesses and other local organizations with everyday technology, networking and audio/video needs.

Can you help if I don't know what the problem is?

Absolutely. Describe the symptom — no sound, slow computer, weak Wi-Fi, blank screen, app errors — and TechEase can start with diagnosis.

Do you offer remote support?

Yes, for issues that can be safely handled without physical access to the equipment. Hardware installation and hands-on troubleshooting require an on-site visit.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Service is available in English and Spanish.
